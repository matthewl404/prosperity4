## Setup

Portfolio allocation problem. Total budget: 1,000,000 XIRECs. Allocate % across 6 tradeable goods, pay fees on each position.

| Good | Side | % | Investment | Fee | P&L |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lava cake | SELL | 10% | 100,000 | 10,000 | **+53,353** |
| Scoria paste | BUY | 18% | 180,000 | 32,400 | -30,001 |
| Magma ink | BUY | 18% | 180,000 | 32,400 | -28,391 |
| Obsidian cutlery | BUY | 12% | 120,000 | 14,400 | -2,501 |
| Sulfur reactor | BUY | 20% | 200,000 | 40,000 | -5,152 |
| Thermalite core | BUY | 22% | 220,000 | 48,400 | +352 |

**Total: Investment 1,000,000 — Fees 177,600 — PnL: -12,339**

---

## Analysis

- Only Lava cake was clearly profitable (+53k). The SELL direction was correct.
- All BUY positions were losers except Thermalite core which barely broke even (+352).
- The fee structure (flat % of investment) heavily penalized large allocations — Scoria paste and Magma ink each paid 32k in fees before generating any return.
- Total fees of 177,600 on a 1M budget = 17.76% fee drag. With that headwind, nearly every position needed a large directional move to be profitable.

## What I'd Change

- Concentrate heavily on the highest-conviction position (Lava cake SELL) instead of spreading across 6 goods.
- With a 10-20% fee structure, fewer but larger bets on correctly-identified goods beats diversification.
- Research which goods have the strongest signals before allocating — BUY bias on 5 of 6 goods when prices were falling was the core mistake.
